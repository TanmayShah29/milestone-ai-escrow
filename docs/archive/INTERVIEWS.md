# Talking to real people: a kit

The landing page says Milestone is for "teams hiring freelancers or AI coding agents". Nobody outside this project
has confirmed that yet. Five short conversations before the video can confirm it, change it, or give you a quote
that judges remember. The Devpost text and README must only quote what people actually said, with their permission.

Aim: 5 conversations of 15 minutes each, by Nov 6. Rough split: 2 people who hire developers or freelancers
(a founder, an agency owner, a tech lead), 2 freelance developers, and 1 person who runs or builds with AI coding
agents.

## Where to find them quickly
- People you already know: classmates, ex-colleagues, anyone who has freelanced on Upwork, Fiverr or Toptal.
- Your college or city developer groups on WhatsApp, Discord or Telegram.
- The PayPal hackathon Discord (https://discord.gg/sJ2G6DyvSK): other builders hire and freelance too.
- r/freelance, r/forhire, or Indie Hackers: ask for a 15-minute call, not for feedback on a product.

## Message to send (edit it into your own words)

> Hi [name], I'm building a hackathon project about getting paid for freelance code work, and I'm trying to learn
> how people handle it today. Could I ask you a few questions for 15 minutes this week? I'm not selling anything.
> It would really help.

## The conversation

Ask about their past, not the product. Let them talk. Show the product only at the end.

1. Tell me about the last time you paid someone, or were paid, for a piece of software work. How did you agree on
   what "done" meant?
2. Did anything go wrong: late payment, no payment, disagreement about whether it was finished? What happened?
3. How did you protect yourself? (Escrow, upfront deposit, milestones, trust, contracts.)
4. How much time did checking the work, or chasing the payment, take?
5. Have you used, or would you use, an AI coding agent for paid work? Would you let it be paid automatically?
6. *Only now:* show https://milestone-ai-escrow.onrender.com/demo and click **Cheating worker**. Ask: what would
   stop you from using something like this? What would make it a must-have?
7. Can I quote you by first name and role (for example "Priya, agency owner")? Is there anything you said that
   I shouldn't use?

## What to write down after each call
- Who they are (role, hires or is hired, how often).
- The most painful story, in their words.
- One sentence you could quote, and whether they agreed to it.
- Whether "teams hiring freelancers or coding agents" fits them. If not, who did they think it was for?
- Their biggest objection.

## Deciding the audience after 5 calls
- If the people who hire felt the pain most, keep the current line.
- If freelancers felt it most, lead with getting paid: change the landing headline to "Get paid when the work
  passes" and the eyebrow to "For freelancers and AI agents doing paid code work".
- If nobody cared, say so in the plan, and lead the video with the AI attack and the agent story instead of the
  market claim.

Tell Claude what you learned and which quotes you have permission for, and it will update the landing page,
README and Devpost text.
