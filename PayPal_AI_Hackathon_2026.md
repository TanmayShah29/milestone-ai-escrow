# PayPal AI Hackathon 2026: "Build What's Next with PayPal and AI"

Source: https://paypalaihackathon.devpost.com/ (managed by Devpost), plus PayPal's blog post (Sept 30, 2026)
Info gathered: Oct 15, 2026

## Quick facts
- Format: global, online, public hackathon
- Participants so far: ~14,500
- Eligibility: above the legal age of majority in your country of residence; all countries/territories except standard exclusions (check the Rules tab for the exact list)
- Team: solo or team
- New or existing projects allowed, as long as you make meaningful progress during the hackathon
- No tracks and no fixed problem statements
- One hard requirement: the project must **meaningfully use both PayPal and AI**

## Dates
- Submissions opened: Oct 1, 2026
- **Submissions close: Nov 12, 2026, 12:00 pm PST = Nov 13, 2026, 1:30 AM IST**
- Winners announced: Dec 21, 2026 (per PayPal's blog)

## Project requirements
- Meaningfully integrate at least one PayPal technology (API, SDK, product, developer capability), using the free PayPal sandbox
- Meaningfully incorporate AI (any AI tool, model, or platform)
- Working prototype or proof of concept
- Documented well enough for judges to evaluate
- Comply with the Official Rules

## What to submit
1. Project built with the required tools
2. Text description of features and functionality
3. Functional demo (mockups, static prototypes and non-functional demos do NOT count). Provide either:
   - clear, complete setup and run instructions in the repo, or
   - a hosted demo URL
4. Tools used (model or AI service) and how they were used
5. Public code repo on GitHub, GitLab or Bitbucket
   - all source code, assets and run instructions
   - public and open source, with a license file visible in the repo's About section
6. Demo video
   - under 3 minutes
   - shows the project working on the device it was built for
   - public on YouTube, link in the submission form
   - no third-party trademarks or copyrighted music/material without permission

## Judging criteria
1. **Technological Implementation**: how thoroughly and skillfully PayPal Developer Platform and AI are used; a working, non-trivial implementation
2. **Design**: a complete, coherent product experience, not just a technical proof of concept
3. **Potential Impact**: a credible, specific case for solving a real problem for a real audience
4. **Innovation/Idea**: creativity and novelty
5. **Presentation**: the video shows the project working end to end; clear pitch of the problem, audience and why it matters
